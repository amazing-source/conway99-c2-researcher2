# Researcher 2 — full existence problem

## Mission

Investigate EX in MODEL.md. Use the independently hand-reviewed T4 as a working lemma, keeping its scope explicit. Do not spend the session simply re-auditing it. Do not assume a positive or negative answer.

Your task is to do mathematics that could decide EX, not to produce another generic list of methods. The theorem is not an oracle that emits an answer: it supplies an exact conditional reconstruction once a complete N and a feasible completion are available.

## Initial independent phase

Read MODEL.md and AUDIT_STATUS.md, not the earlier discovery history.
Write a short work/STARTING_POINT.md stating:

1. The exact target you are working on, with its quantifiers.
2. What is fixed, what is unknown and what is already a theorem.
3. Your independently derived initial consequences and up to three candidate mechanisms.
4. For each mechanism, how success would constrain EX, and what main implication it would still need.

Then select one and start deriving results. Do not ask the operator to supply mathematical taste. Before a substantial investment, read DUPLICATION_NOTES.md and check whether the proposed implication is already known or was invalidated. This is staged information, not a promise of perfect cognitive isolation.

## Permit genuine invention without novelty theatre

New definitions, alternative representations and old tools used in a new proof are all allowed. A useful lemma need not be logically independent of the old equations: consequences can make a hidden contradiction accessible.
Do not introduce elaborate vocabulary without an explicit map from the exact object and a theorem using it.
Do not assume that all low-order, spectral, lattice or modular methods have been exhausted merely because specific earlier attempts failed.

## Full candidate scope

Work with an arbitrary full (N,D) unless a precisely defined subfamily is declared. No symmetry is known here to be forced.
A symmetry ansatz is legitimate for a bounded positive construction attempt: one successful witness settles existence. Its failure only excludes that ansatz. It cannot be presented as a universal negative result.
Likewise, a classification is a proof route only with an exhaustive partition and sound coverage. State the missing coverage lemma if a family-level argument is all that is obtained.

You may investigate the stronger assertion S=empty, but do not use completion-only assumptions while claiming to prove that stronger assertion.

## Method choice

No particular route is mandated: do not mechanically repeat the parity-defect proof, eliminate D by fiat, or start a ternary/lattice/symmetry programme just because another agent preferred it.
Compare routes by a concrete invariant, reconstruction rule, universal certificate, forced structure or rigorous reduction they could deliver.
Do not claim that row enumeration is cheap or that the whole N-space is tractable without a bounded measurement and a coverage/cost argument. A small number of row types does not by itself bound the work of mutually compatible row assemblies.

## Computation and access

Short, exact local tests may falsify a lemma, validate an identity or assess a sharply defined construction. Record exact inputs, tested assertions, limits and verdicts. A numerical or modular approximation is not an exact witness unless exact reconstruction and verification follow.
No cloud launches, paid services, messages to third parties, quota requests, repository writes or large/background searches are authorized. One local process; at most five minutes per check with a stated memory bound. Do not evade the limit by launching an unbounded series of runs.

Source lookup is allowed after the initial independent note and when it has a mathematical purpose, including novelty checks and verifying cited external inputs. Use primary mathematical sources and record what information came from them. Do not regard a missing keyword hit as proof that an idea is new.

## Positive and negative evidence

For a positive candidate, save exact N,D, construct A and verify every defining graph condition and the involution directly. The included checker is a convenience, not an independent substitute for your own verification.
For a negative result, write the implication from arbitrary admissible (N,D) to contradiction. Every extra condition must be derived or assigned a defined subcase. Explicitly state the remaining cases.
For a new useful necessary condition, include a proof and explain whether it (i) eliminates a specified family, (ii) gives a reduction with proved coverage, or (iii) still requires an additional bridge. Do not report sampled failure percentages as candidate-space elimination.

## Ongoing record and final handoff

Keep work/RESULTS.md, work/FAILED_ATTEMPTS.md and work/CHECKPOINT.md.
Use labels: PROVED HERE (written proof), CHECKED EXAMPLE/COMPUTATION, CONJECTURE, EXTERNAL INPUT, OPEN.

For each substantial result record:

- precise statement and hypotheses;
- complete proof or exact unresolved step;
- strongest counterexample test performed and its scope;
- direct consequence for EX;
- what remains after using it.

If the investigation does not settle EX, return the strongest actual result, the exact missing implication and why the chosen route remains worth pursuing or should be abandoned. Do not substitute a list of plausible techniques or claims of being close.

Start the independent phase and then carry out the selected mathematical investigation.
